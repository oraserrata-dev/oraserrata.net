About

The Studio

Ora Serrata is a one-person software studio building apps for Apple devices. Each app starts with an everyday task that could be easier.

Fovea grew out of a simple frustration: a screenshot on the clipboard should be easy to crop and save. Rouse makes it easier to wake a computer when you need it.

The Name

The ora serrata is the serrated front edge of the retina. The studio takes its name from that small detail of the eye.

Our Apps

Rouse: a Wake-on-LAN app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Wake compatible computers on your local network, or connect to a relay over a VPN when you are away. Currently in development.

Fovea: a Mac app that saves copied images, PDFs, and text in the format you choose. Crop images, edit text, and convert Markdown locally. Coming soon to the Mac App Store.
