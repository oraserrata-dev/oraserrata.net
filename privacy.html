Ora Serrata LLC ("we," "us," or "our") builds apps that respect your privacy. This policy covers our apps, Fovea and Rouse, the optional Rouse Relay server software, this website, and support correspondence.

Fovea

Fovea (Fovea: Clipboard Saver) processes copied images, PDFs, and text on your Mac. It does not upload your clipboard or file contents to Ora Serrata or to an online conversion service. Fovea requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Clipboard access. Fovea reads the clipboard when you bring the app to the foreground and checks for changes while it is active. It uses this content to provide previews, cropping, text editing, conversion, saving, and copying back to the clipboard. It does not monitor the clipboard in the background or maintain a clipboard history.

What stays on your Mac. Fovea remembers your selected export formats, the paths of up to ten recently saved files, and permission to access the Quick Save folder you choose. These preferences contain locations and settings, not a saved history of your clipboard contents. Fovea does not send them to us or provide its own cloud sync.

Files you save or share. Saved files go to the destination you choose. Copy to Clipboard places your result on the macOS clipboard, and dragging a file into another app gives that app access to it. If you choose an iCloud-synced folder, a network drive, another app, or a macOS clipboard-sharing feature, that destination or service may transfer or retain the content according to its own settings and policies.

Retention and deletion. Fovea holds the current preview and unsaved edits in memory during use; it does not restore them as a clipboard history after quitting. You can clear the recent-file list using File → Recent Saves → Clear Recents. Clearing this list does not delete the files themselves. Saved files remain until you delete them, and other preferences remain locally until you remove the app's stored settings. Removing the app alone may leave settings and exported files behind.

Your controls. Quit Fovea to stop clipboard access. Where your version of macOS provides a clipboard permission control, you can also revoke access in System Settings. File and folder access is limited by the macOS App Sandbox and the locations you authorize. Contact us if you need help removing local settings.

The Rouse-specific sections below describe that app and its optional relay. Support email, this website, Apple diagnostics, children's privacy, your rights, policy changes, and contact information apply as described to both apps and their users.

Information We Collect

Rouse is designed to collect as little data as possible, and to keep what it does hold on devices you control. None of the following ever reaches a server operated by Ora Serrata:

  • Device configuration you enter: names, MAC addresses, broadcast IP addresses, and SecureON passwords (if used) for the devices you want to wake
  • Network identifiers for the Wi-Fi networks you save as Locations: SSID and BSSID
  • Relay configuration you enter: relay hostname, port, and auth token, if you set up the optional Rouse Relay

This information is stored locally on your device and, if you have iCloud sync enabled, synced through Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage to your other Apple devices signed in to the same Apple Account. We have no access to it.

Information We Do Not Collect

Rouse itself does not collect the following information for Ora Serrata:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any other personal identifier
  • Your precise geographic location
  • Usage analytics, behavioral data, or telemetry
  • Advertising identifiers
  • The contents of any wake request you send

Permissions Rouse Requests

The app asks for the following operating-system permissions. Each is requested only when the relevant feature first needs it, never at app launch.

  • Local Network (iOS): required to broadcast Wake-on-LAN magic packets on your Wi-Fi.
  • Location When In Use (iOS and macOS): Apple gates access to the current Wi-Fi network's name and BSSID behind the Location permission. Rouse uses this only to read which Wi-Fi you're connected to so it can organize devices by Location. Your precise geographic location is never accessed or stored.
  • Notifications: used to confirm wake results and surface routing errors. You can disable this in system Settings at any time.

iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud sync enabled inside Rouse (the default), your device list and Location list are synced across your Apple devices using Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage service. This data is stored in your private Apple Account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Ora Serrata does not have access to your iCloud account or its contents.

To stop syncing you can disable iCloud sync inside Rouse's Settings, or remove Rouse's data from iCloud in your device's Settings → Apple ID → iCloud.

Rouse Relay (Self-Hosted)

Rouse Relay is optional server software you can run on your own always-on device (a Mac, a Raspberry Pi, a NAS, a Docker host, or similar) so the app can wake your devices when you're away from home. Ora Serrata does not operate a hosted relay service. Every relay is run by its user on their own hardware, on their own network.

Rouse on macOS can also take this role itself, through Relay Mode in its settings, instead of running the separate server. Both handle wake requests exactly as described below, and in both cases the relay runs on your own hardware and the requests stay on your own network.

Each wake request sent to your relay contains only:

  • The target device's MAC address
  • The broadcast IP address and UDP port for the local network
  • An optional SecureON password if your device requires one
  • Your relay auth token in the request header, for authentication

The relay does not persist wake requests after they are processed. It writes operational log entries (events like "Wake sent" or "Bad token") to its standard output for your own troubleshooting; these never leave the device you run the relay on.

Support Email

If you email hello@oraserrata.net, we receive your email address, your message, and any name or attachments you include. We use this information to reply and investigate the issue you raise. Our email provider processes and stores the correspondence to provide email service.

We keep support correspondence unless you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, email the same address. Please send only the information needed for support and remove private content from any screenshots or sample files you choose to share. Fovea does not automatically send us its clipboard contents with a support request.

This Website

Visiting oraserrata.net is separate from using Fovea's local conversion features. This website is served through Cloudflare and uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits and page performance. Cloudflare processes technical request information to deliver and protect the site. Its Web Analytics service reports information such as pages visited, referring sites, browser and device type, country, and loading performance, without using analytics cookies.

The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts. To deliver them, Google receives requests from your browser, including your IP address and browser information. These website services do not receive Fovea's clipboard contents merely because you visit this site or open its privacy policy.

For the providers' handling and retention of website information, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy, Cloudflare's Web Analytics documentation, and Google Fonts' privacy information. You can contact us with questions about website data or privacy requests.

Diagnostics Shared with Apple

If you have opted in to "Share With App Developers" under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements on your device, Apple may make crash and performance reports available to us to help diagnose problems. We do not run any third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. This channel is entirely Apple-mediated and you can turn it off at any time in your device's settings.

Third-Party Services

Rouse contains no third-party analytics, advertising frameworks, tracking SDKs, or social-login integrations. The only network connections the app makes are:

  • UDP broadcasts on your local network, to wake target devices
  • HTTP or HTTPS requests to your own self-hosted Rouse Relay, if you have configured one
  • Apple's iCloud service, mediated entirely by the operating system, for the sync described above

Data Retention and Deletion

Because Rouse stores data only on your device and in your iCloud account, you control retention and deletion completely:

  • To delete data on a single device, uninstall the Rouse app from that device.
  • To delete iCloud-synced data, open Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on iOS, or System Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage on macOS, and remove Rouse.
  • The optional Rouse Relay does not retain wake requests; stopping or uninstalling the relay removes its data immediately.
