Rouse

Wake your devices from anywhere.

Rouse is a Wake-on-LAN app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac that lets you power on your computers remotely, whether you're across the room or across the world.

Local & remote wake
Send magic packets on your LAN, or wake devices over the internet through a lightweight relay.
iCloud sync
Your device list stays in sync across all your Apple devices, automatically.
Widgets & Shortcuts
Wake a device from your Home Screen, Lock Screen, or with Siri, no app launch required.
Built for Apple
Native SwiftUI on every platform. Designed to feel right at home on iOS, iPadOS, and macOS.

Fovea

Clip it, convert it, file it.

Fovea is your clipboard's companion: a small, focused app that turns whatever you copy into a file, in the format you choose. Screenshots, PDFs, and AI-written Markdown become the file format you actually need, entirely on your Mac. Nothing you copy ever leaves it.

Every format you need
Images save as PNG, JPEG, PDF, HEIC, or TIFF. PDFs stay PDF or rasterize to an image. Text saves as plain text, Markdown, rich text, or HTML. A live hint shows the estimated file size before you commit.
Built for the AI era
Answers from AI assistants arrive as Markdown. Fovea converts it locally: render Markdown into rich text for Word or email, into clean HTML for your site or newsletter, or strip the syntax entirely for a plain-text paste.
Three ways to save
Save As gives you the full panel: name it, place it, every time. Quick Save writes instantly to a folder you choose once. Or drag the preview straight into Finder, Mail, or Slack as a finished file.
Private by design
Fovea reads the clipboard only while it's open, and only to show you the preview. It never monitors in the background, keeps no history of what you copy, has no network access, and collects nothing.
