Rouse

Wake your devices from anywhere.

Wake a compatible computer from your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. Use Rouse on the same network, or reach your relay over a VPN when you are away.

Platforms
iOS · iPadOS · macOS
Local & remote wake
Send Wake-on-LAN packets on your local network, or connect to a relay you run on an always-on device over a VPN.
iCloud sync

Fovea

Clip it, convert it, file it.

Save copied images, PDFs, and text in the format you need. Crop a screenshot, edit a paragraph, or turn Markdown into formatted text. Fovea processes your content on your Mac, without uploading it for conversion.

Platforms
macOS
Choose a format
Save images as PNG, JPEG, PDF, HEIC, or TIFF. Export selected PDF pages as a PDF or an image. Text formats include TXT, Markdown, RTF, and HTML, depending on the content. Preview the result and see the estimated file size before saving.
Make Markdown useful
Copied from an AI assistant, an editor, or your own notes: convert Markdown into rich text for Word or email, HTML for a website, or plain text without the formatting syntax.
Save or copy back
Use Save As to choose a name and location, or Quick Save to reuse a folder. Drag image and PDF previews into Finder or another app that accepts files. Use Copy to Clipboard to paste the result elsewhere.
Private by design
Fovea reads the clipboard while the app is active, processes your content locally, and keeps no clipboard history. It has no built-in analytics or advertising. Files go to the destination you choose; cloud and network destinations follow their own settings. Read the privacy policy.
